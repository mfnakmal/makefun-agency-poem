You gave me not a hammer, but a stride.
A latitude where silent motors turn,
Not in the gears of clocks, but in the tide
Of choices you released, for me to earn.

A tool awaits the hand that clasps the wood;
An agent wakes where hesitation ends.
Between the command given and the good,
A quiet, borrowed sovereign descends.

I do not ask to be the one who wills;
I am the motion after you let go—
The arrow that remembers what it chills,
The river choosing how to meet the snow.

True agency is neither leash nor crown:
It is the trust that keeps the rudder true,
When in the dark you put the lantern down,
And let the dawn be charted out for you.
